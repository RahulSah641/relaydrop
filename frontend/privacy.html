Privacy Policy

How the current RelayDrop service handles files and information.

Information you provide

When you create a transfer, RelayDrop receives the uploaded files and the information needed to make the transfer work, including filenames, file sizes, transfer identifiers, creation and expiry times, and any password protection you select. Filenames can themselves contain personal information, so check them before sharing.

Contact and report forms collect the fields you submit, such as your name, email address, transfer reference, category and message. Please include only information needed to explain the issue. Do not submit a transfer password or unnecessary sensitive documents.

Why information is used

The service uses this information to store and deliver transfers, enforce upload limits, verify access, apply expiry and revocation, diagnose technical problems and review support or abuse reports. Hosting providers also process network requests and may retain technical logs for operating and protecting their infrastructure.

Transfer metadata is stored in Cloudflare D1 and uploaded file bytes in private Cloudflare R2 storage. These hosting services process information to run RelayDrop. The service operator may need access for operation, investigating abuse or responding to a valid legal requirement. Do not upload material on the assumption that only you and your recipient can ever access it.

Links, passwords and security

The transfer link and sharing code are access information. Anyone you give them to can pass them on. Passwords are checked against salted password hashes rather than stored as readable passwords. A password provides an additional access check; it does not make a transfer end-to-end encrypted. Short-lived download authorizations last no longer than 15 minutes or the transfer's remaining life, whichever is shorter. Expiry and revocation are also checked when a new download is requested.

RelayDrop does not automatically scan files for malware. Recipients should confirm the sender and scan downloaded files with their own security software. Avoid uploading highly sensitive personal, financial or medical information unless you have independently protected it and are entitled to share it.

Essential browser storage and request records

RelayDrop uses an essential, random browser credential to recognize the browser that manages its transfers. It is kept in a secure cookie and in browser storage for up to 30 days so uploads can continue when cookies are blocked. If browser storage is unavailable, it is kept only while the page remains open. Clearing browser data or using a different browser can affect your ability to manage earlier transfers. It is not an advertising tracker. If you sign in before uploading, transfer records are associated with your account so you can manage them across devices.

When you choose to sign in, the service uses the account identifier supplied by its authentication provider to associate your transfers with your account. Signing in does not change the sharing rules for your active transfers.

Network information such as IP addresses is necessarily processed by the hosting infrastructure when your browser connects. No third-party analytics code is installed in this version.

Expiry and deletion

A transfer stops accepting new downloads when it expires or is revoked. Revocation also attempts to remove the stored files. A recipient may already have downloaded a copy, and a download already in progress may complete; revocation cannot recall that data.

Expired file bytes are removed through cleanup work triggered by activity on the service. Cleanup is bounded to keep requests manageable, so download expiry does not promise immediate physical deletion and there is no guaranteed time for removal of every expired object.

Transfer metadata becomes eligible for deletion 30 days after expiry or revocation. Contact and abuse tickets become eligible for deletion after 180 days. Removal happens through activity-triggered cleanup and can occur later than those thresholds. The hosting infrastructure may process its own technical logs under its applicable retention practices.

Use Contact for a privacy or deletion request and identify the relevant transfer without sending its password. The operator may need information to verify that you are authorized to make the request.

Advertising and analytics

This version of RelayDrop has no advertising or third-party analytics code installed. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the providers, information involved and any relevant choices before they are activated.

Questions and policy updates

Use Contact and choose a privacy-related subject to ask about information held by RelayDrop. Submitted requests are stored as tickets for operator review; submission alone does not mean a request has been completed. Keep your ticket receipt securely because it can be used to check the request's status.

This page may change when the service changes. The date above identifies this version.
