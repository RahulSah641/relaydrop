Copyright & Abuse Policy

Report a transfer that may infringe rights or put someone at risk.

Share only what you are allowed to share

RelayDrop is intended for authorized file transfers. It must not be used to distribute infringing works, malware, stolen information or other prohibited material. The sender is responsible for having permission to share the uploaded files.

How to make a useful copyright report

Open Report abuse and select Copyright. Identify the transfer using its link or eight-digit code. Explain which file is at issue and which original work or rights you believe are affected. A public reference to the original work can help distinguish it from another work with a similar title.

Give your name and a contact email, and explain whether you are the rights holder or are authorized to act for them.

Identify the protected work and the relevant transfer and filename as precisely as possible.

Explain why you believe the sharing is unauthorized, including any relevant context or permission information.

Confirm that the report is accurate to the best of your knowledge and made in good faith.

Include your typed name as a signature for the report.

Other abuse reports

Choose the closest report category and describe the problem without reposting the harmful material. Do not upload an additional copy of illegal content as evidence. If there is an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact the appropriate emergency service; the RelayDrop form is not an emergency response service.

What happens after submission

The form stores a ticket for operator review. The operator may restrict access, remove a transfer or request more information as appropriate. A ticket reference confirms receipt by the system, not a finding about the report or a completed removal.

If your transfer was restricted and you believe the decision was mistaken, use Contact with the transfer reference and your explanation. Do not reupload the same reported material simply to bypass a restriction.

Information in a report

Reports can contain personal information. Provide only what is needed to investigate. Information may need to be used or disclosed when assessing a claim or responding to a legal requirement; do not assume the report is confidential.

This page explains RelayDrop's reporting process. It does not represent a court decision, a guarantee of removal or a claim that RelayDrop has a registered DMCA agent.
