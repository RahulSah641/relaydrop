Terms of Service

The ground rules for using RelayDrop.

The service

RelayDrop provides temporary file transfers. By using it, you agree to these terms and to use the service only for lawful purposes. You are responsible for deciding whether this type of temporary hosting is suitable for your files and recipients.

A transfer can contain up to 20 files, no more than 50 MiB each and 100 MiB combined. You choose an expiry of one hour, 24 hours, three days or seven days. Expired, revoked or removed transfers are unavailable for download. Technical problems can also interrupt access, so keep a separate copy of anything important.

Your files and permission to handle them

You retain any rights you hold in your files. Uploading a file gives RelayDrop permission to receive, store and deliver it as needed to provide the transfer service and address abuse or legal requirements.

You must have the right to upload and share the material. A file being available online, or your having purchased a copy, does not by itself give you permission to redistribute it. Obtain any consent needed from people whose personal information appears in a file.

Unacceptable use

Do not share infringing copies of books, films, music, software or other protected material.

Do not upload malware, stolen credentials, phishing material or files intended to compromise another device.

Do not share illegal sexual material, non-consensual intimate imagery, exploitative content, threats or another person's private information without authority.

Do not use the service for fraud, harassment, evading access controls or concealing unlawful activity.

Do not probe sharing codes, bypass limits, automate abusive traffic or interfere with the service.

Access and your responsibilities

Protect the sharing link, code and optional password. A sharing code is a locator, not a guarantee of confidentiality. To revoke a transfer, use the browser that created it. Transfers uploaded while signed in can also be managed through that account on another device.

Transfers are not end-to-end encrypted, and uploads are not automatically scanned for malware. Check files before sending them and verify files before opening them. Revoking a transfer cannot recover copies that recipients have already downloaded.

Reports and removal

Use Report abuse for a suspected violation, and include enough information to locate the transfer and understand the issue. RelayDrop may disable access or remove a transfer when reviewing abuse, maintaining the service or responding to a valid legal requirement.

Reports are reviewed based on the information available. Submitting a report does not automatically establish that a violation occurred, and reports must be accurate and made in good faith.

Availability and updates

The service is provided as available. Transfer availability, uninterrupted operation and recovery of lost files are not guaranteed. These terms do not remove any rights that applicable law says cannot be excluded.

Features and these terms may change. The date above identifies the current version. Use Contact if you have a question about these terms.
